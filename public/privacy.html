Privacy Policy

Workloat · Last updated: July 18, 2026

Workloat ("Workloat," "we," "us") is a personal-finance app that helps you track your purchases, subscriptions, refunds, returns, and upcoming charges. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Workloat mobile app and related services (the "Service"), operated by Workloat.

We built Workloat around a simple principle: your financial data is used only to power features you see in the app. We never sell it and never use it for advertising.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and Google account identifier to create and secure your account. Authentication is handled by Google and by our backend provider (Supabase).

Purchase emails (Gmail) — only if you connect Gmail

If you choose to connect your Google account with read access to Gmail (gmail.readonly), Workloat scans only purchase-related messages (order confirmations, receipts, invoices, subscription and BNPL notices) to extract structured details: merchant, amount, dates, category, and return/refund/renewal information. We store only these extracted fields — we do not retain the raw content of your emails. Your Google authorization token is stored securely on our servers and is never placed on your device.

Google API disclosure. Workloat's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Gmail data for advertising, do not sell it, do not transfer it except as needed to provide or improve user-facing features (or for security, legal, or with your consent), and do not allow humans to read it except with your affirmative consent, for security/abuse purposes, or where required by law.

Bank and card transactions (Plaid) — only if you connect a bank

If you link a financial account, we use Plaid Inc. to connect to your bank or card issuer and retrieve transaction history (merchant, amount, date, and category). You authorize this connection through Plaid's own secure flow; your bank login credentials are handled by Plaid and are never seen or stored by Workloat. The long-lived access token Plaid issues is stored only on our servers, never on your device. Plaid's handling of your information is governed by the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.

Apple Wallet transactions (FinanceKit) — on your device

If you enable Apple Wallet sync, Workloat reads eligible card transactions from the Wallet app entirely on your device, with your permission, using Apple's FinanceKit. This data is processed locally to display your transactions and detect subscriptions; it is never transmitted to our servers or to any third party, and it is stored in an encrypted, app-private location on your device.

Information you enter

Purchases, notes, and profile details (such as your name) that you add manually.

Limited technical information

Basic diagnostic and reliability information needed to operate the app (for example, error logs). We do not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the core features: showing purchases, subscriptions, spending, return windows, refunds, and upcoming charges.
  • To detect recurring subscriptions and surface money at risk.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To operate, maintain, debug, and improve the Service.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your financial or email data for advertising or to build advertising profiles.

Automated processing to extract details

To turn a receipt or transaction into structured data (merchant, amount, kind), we use automated parsing. Where this involves a third-party AI/model provider, we send only the minimum content needed for extraction, under agreements that prohibit using your data to train their models and, where available, provide zero data retention. This applies to email content processing only; Apple Wallet data is never sent off your device.

3. How we share information

We share information only with service providers that help us run the Service, and only as needed to provide it:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and backend hosting (data stored encrypted at rest).
  • Google — sign-in and, if connected, Gmail access.
  • Plaid — if you link a bank/card.
  • Brandfetch — to display merchant brand logos; we send only a merchant's website domain, never your amounts, dates, or account details.
  • An AI/model provider, if used, for the email parsing described above.

We may also disclose information to comply with the law or a valid legal request, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a merger or acquisition (with notice to you). We never sell your personal information.

4. Data storage and security

  • Data in transit is encrypted with TLS 1.2 or higher.
  • Data at rest on our servers is encrypted (AES-256 via Supabase).
  • On-device data is protected by iOS Data Protection (encrypted at rest).
  • Sensitive credentials (your Google and Plaid access tokens) are stored server-side only and are not readable by the app or other users; access is restricted by row-level security and least-privilege controls.
  • No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information using industry-standard safeguards.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You can:

  • Disconnect Gmail or a linked bank at any time from the app or your Google account settings, which stops further data collection from that source.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing hello@payloat.com. When you delete your account, we delete your stored purchases, connection tokens, and profile data; related records are removed automatically (cascading deletion), subject to limited retention where required by law.

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (including under GDPR and the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these, contact us at hello@payloat.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

7. Children's privacy

Workloat is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International users

We operate the Service from, and store data in, the United States. If you access Workloat from outside the U.S., you understand your information may be processed in the U.S., where data-protection laws may differ from those in your country.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here with an updated date and, for material changes, provide additional notice within the app.
